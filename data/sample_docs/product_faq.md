# Brightleaf Insights Product FAQ

## What is Brightleaf Insights

Brightleaf Insights is the company's fictional dashboard product. It connects to a data warehouse and turns SQL queries into shareable dashboards.

## Pricing

The Starter plan costs 2,000 INR per user per month and includes up to 10 dashboards.

The Team plan costs 4,500 INR per user per month and includes unlimited dashboards and scheduled email reports.

The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds single sign-on (SSO), audit logs and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial.

## Integrations

Brightleaf Insights supports PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake and BigQuery as data sources.

Dashboards can be exported to PDF and PNG. Alerts can be sent to Slack and Microsoft Teams.

## Data Retention and Support

Query history is kept for 90 days on Starter and Team plans and for 1 year on Enterprise.

Support is available by email on all plans. Enterprise customers also get a dedicated customer success manager.

The support team responds to priority-one incidents within 1 hour for Enterprise customers.
